Kakobuy shopping guide

A better shortlist starts with four checks.

01

Choose your category

Begin with shoes, a layer or an accessory. Keep the first comparison focused on products with the same purpose.

02

Read the actual listing

Review the selected variation, measurements, stated materials and seller terms. Save the exact product reference.

03

Confirm service support

If using a shopping service, confirm that it accepts the exact URL and item. Do not assume every linked product is supported.

04

Compare the full cost

Consider delivery, service charges, currency conversion and applicable taxes alongside the item price.

Your comparison framework

What belongs in your spreadsheet?

These fields turn a collection of product links into a useful decision tool.

Suggested fields for a personal fashion comparison sheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Product reference Exact URL, item name, selected color and variation Keeps similar-looking listings separate.
Fit and sizing Relevant measurements, size chart and your comparison garment Reduces reliance on inconsistent size labels.
Materials Seller-stated composition and any missing information Highlights questions to resolve before payment.
Photo evidence Available detail images and what they actually show Separates visible features from unverified claims.
Estimated cost Item price, delivery, fees and possible taxes, with a check date Supports a more realistic budget comparison.
Order conditions Availability, service support and applicable return terms Flags practical limitations before ordering.

Budget formula: item price + domestic delivery, if charged + service fees + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties. Include currency conversion and optional services where relevant. Confirm current charges before paying.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated list of product references intended to help shoppers organize and compare finds. Entries may include links, categories, measurements, prices and notes. A listing alone does not verify authenticity, availability or seller reliability.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent fashion discovery guide that links to CAFANS. It is not operated by or endorsed by Kakobuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

No downloadable spreadsheet is provided here. This page offers category links and a comparison guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS.

Does a product link guarantee Kakobuy compatibility?

No. Check whether the shopping service accepts the exact product URL and item before paying. Product restrictions, supported sources and availability may change.

How should I compare clothing and sneaker sizes?

Use the individual listing’s measurements and size chart. Compare clothing measurements with a garment you own and check the stated foot-length guidance for footwear. Do not assume that the same size label fits identically across products.

How do I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add the item price, any domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Currency conversion and optional services can add costs. Confirm a current quote before ordering.
